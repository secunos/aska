**Tag:** `v1.2.0` (annotated, by the release owner `secunos`; the tag commit is the one built, `5f7d962`). **Built:** 16 October 2026, 07:28 UTC. **Status:** feature release on top of 1.1.0 — the 1.2 plan (`docs/Aska_1.2_Plan_v0.2.md`) steps 1–6: **the paper mode** (`docs/Aska_Design_Change_DC-04_Paper_Mode_v0.3.md`, Decision Record D-23). **Not independently audited and not legally reviewed** — see `SECURITY.md`, the User Guide §1 and §9; the owner's decision of 7 October 2026 that neither review is planned stands.

## What changed since 1.1.0

1. **One-time pad booklets (DC-04 §3–§4; core `crates/aska-paper`, step 2).** Text to digits by a fixed public straddling checkerboard; digit-wise addition modulo 10; pages of 200, 400 or 600 pad digits with row check digits, a set code, an A/B direction and a page number; an all-digit canonical string whose SHA3-256 (mod 10⁶) is the printed checksum and whose numeric-mode QR is the device path's input. Two integrity tags: a four-digit **hand tag** (a one-time linear hash modulo 9 973 over two-digit groups, computed by a person) and a nineteen-digit **device tag** (Horner modulo 2⁶¹ − 1, checked by the app). Randomness from the camera's sensor noise through SP 800-90B health tests, a conservative min-entropy estimate and a seeded Toeplitz extractor with a 128-bit margin, mixed with the operating system's generator (**PHYSICAL**), or from die rolls or keyboard timing seeding SHAKE256 (**SEEDED**) — the label is printed on every page. Every digit, key and tag lives in locked memory; the crate forbids `unsafe` and adds no dependency. A Python reference and test vectors (`reference/aska_paper_ref.py`, `tv_paper.json`) check the Rust.
2. **Command line (step 3).** `aska paper generate | encipher | decipher | check-page | worksheet | cover | seal-cards | open-cards | share-cards`, with sheets to `--ps`, `--pbm`, `--rows` (the terminal, one row per keypress) or `--print PRINTER`. Tags are verified before anything is shown.
3. **Graphical client (step 4).** A **Paper** page: make a booklet (camera, die rolls on an on-screen pad, or keyboard timing), show it row by row in the locked viewer for hand copy, or print it; use a page (scan or type it; encipher or decipher; *Done* destroys it). Block cards through the Send page's **Paper** level and the Receive page's **Read Block cards…**; Share cards from the Hand-over screen.
4. **The printing rule (DC-04 §5).** A pad page is printed only when the print spool is on volatile storage (tmpfs/ramfs, or Tails' amnesic root), the printer is on USB and the user has acknowledged the rules; Share cards to USB printers only; Block cards and the worksheet anywhere. **The toolkit's print operation is not used** — it was found to spool the job through a temporary file — so both clients build PostScript in locked memory and submit it to the local print system over its socket themselves. The guide's printer order follows the dissolving-paper finding: hand copy with water-soluble ink first, dye inkjet second, monochrome laser last and only with the toner step (toner floats off dissolving paper as a legible film); never a colour laser or a network printer.
5. **Worksheet and guide (step 5).** The worksheet as three printable sheets — the method, the worked example with its answers, blank working grids — in both clients; User Guide 1.2 with chapter 11 "Paper mode". The owner's pen-and-paper trial (the worked example blind; a real page copied, enciphered, deciphered and tag-checked by hand and cross-checked on the device) passed with no findings.
6. **Gates.** New: `scripts/no-file-writes-lib.sh aska-paper` (the crate's tests under `strace`), the paper flow in `no-file-writes-cli.sh`, `scripts/memory-gate-paper.sh` (the page QR read off the screen; whole pad, row, grouped row and payload searched in the process — zero after *Forget*), a Ghostscript round trip of the PostScript, `reference/test_paper.py`. Test count 193 + 2 ignored (1.1.0: 131 + 2).

**Not in 1.2:** splitting a booklet between couriers, the single-board reader (RM-07), class-3 Blocks on paper, importing cards from image files (Plan v0.2 §"Not in 1.2"; DC-04 §9). The Send editor and the paper message entry remain GTK text views (C-02 for input).

**Compatibility.** Notes, Key Cards, Shares, Receiving Keys and profiles made by 1.0.x and 1.1.0 work with 1.2.0 and the other way round. The Block Format (draft 0.8: no byte changes), the wire protocol (ADP/1 draft 0.4) and the relay are unchanged; relay operators need not upgrade. Pad pages and cards are paper artefacts outside the Block Format; a Block on a card is byte-identical to a Block on a relay.

## Deviations and known issues

1. **Live camera path and the PHYSICAL source unvalidated on hardware (T-31, T-35).** The smoke test ran on a machine without a camera, as for 1.1.0; the health tests, the estimator and the extractor are tested on synthetic sources only. The first report from a machine with a webcam — a booklet reaching (or not reaching) the PHYSICAL label — is the validation; a SEEDED booklet is computational by declaration and labelled so, and dice work without any camera.
2. **Paper rules are stated, not enforced (T-32, T-34).** Reuse by hand, photographs, impressions and a kept booklet are the holders' discipline; the guide says so.
3. **Printing leaves records (T-33).** The print system's logs and the printer's counter record that a job was printed even under the rule; Block cards are attributable to the printer that made them.
4. **Live CUPS submission not exercised for 1.2.0.** There is no CUPS in CI and the smoke-test machine has no print system, so the IPP path over the CUPS socket (printer listing, `Print-Job`) has run only against its unit tests; the IPP coding, the printer policy and the spool check are unit-tested and the PostScript is verified end to end with Ghostscript. The first `aska paper worksheet --print` on a machine with a printer is its validation; a pad is refused before any digit is generated when the rule does not hold.
5. **Two informational advisories** on `lru 0.12.5` (via `rqrr`) — unchanged from 1.1.0, accepted.
6. **Not built in the release container**; **no Rekor entry**; **reviews not planned** — as for 1.0.x and 1.1.0 (D-19, D-20, D-21).
7. **Design documents:** re-issued at the tag — Decision Record v0.6, Client Design v0.8, Block Format draft 0.8, DC-04 v0.3, Release Checklist v0.3, Plan 1.2 v0.2 — and included in the tagged source. ADP/1 draft 0.4 unchanged.
