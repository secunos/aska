# Aska 1.3 — Plan: Aska on a phone (Android)

## Version 0.1 — 18 October 2026 (for the owner's approval)

- **What this is:** the work list for release 1.3, following the owner's decision of 9 October 2026 (Plan 1.2, "After 1.2": mobile clients in the order GrapheneOS → a standard Samsung phone → iPhone), the mobile pre-study (*Research 08*, 16 October) and the owner's acceptance of its ten recommendations on 16 October 2026 with the iPhone deferred ("accept, iPhone deferred"). The design is *Design Change DC-05 — Aska on a phone v0.1*, delivered with this plan as step 1; this plan is approved together with it or after it.
- **What 1.3 is:** **one Android client** built on the Rust core through a new `aska-ffi` crate — the reference platform **GrapheneOS** on a Pixel, the same APK **validated and documented on a Samsung phone** — with Tor through **Orbot**, the Session closing on screen lock, two memory tiers under Android's 64 KiB lock budget, a fatal-signal handler instead of crash files, `FLAG_SECURE`, no profile and no file writes at all, the paper mode's device path and cards, brand 2.0 on Android, a reproducible signed APK with its certificate fingerprint published, and the mobile gates in CI and on the two phones. **No iPhone code** (1.4, after the owner's decision Q-2).
- **Order of work:** measurements first (a spike week on real hardware — the project will not repeat the camera-without-a-camera situation of 1.1/1.2), then the design approval, then the FFI crate and the build and gate machinery, then the client on GrapheneOS, then the Samsung validation, then guide, specifications and release. Each step ends with its gate green and an update archive in `Code\`; nothing is pushed without the owner.
- **Compatibility:** nothing in 1.3 changes the Block Format, the wire protocol or the relay. A phone seals with KDF profile 2 (reserved since draft 0.4) and opens profiles 1 and 2; the desktop clients, which MAY open profile 2 today, MUST from 1.3 — a one-line change with no byte in the Block. Notes, Key Cards, Receiving Keys, Shares and pad pages travel between phones and desktops unchanged.

## Design decisions (settled; DC-05 §3 and §12)

The ten decisions of Research 08 §9 were accepted on 16 October 2026 (Q-1 release-page APK only; Q-2 iPhone deferred; Q-3 Orbot, no Arti; Q-4 deferred with Q-2; Q-5 close on lock; Q-6 no profile; Q-7 in-app keyboard default, platform keyboard optional; Q-8 device path and cards only; Q-9 two phones; Q-10 1.3 Android, 1.4 iPhone). DC-05 §12 adds four for approval with this plan:

| # | Question | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Q-11 | In-app keyboard layout | One fixed layout: QWERTY with å ä ö, digits, the note's punctuation, shift and a symbols page; no layout settings. |
| Q-12 | APK signing key | A separate key from the minisign release key, same custody (the owner's VM; never on a shared folder; never in CI); certificate fingerprint published with every release; v3 signing with rotation prepared. |
| Q-13 | Which Pixel | The current "a" model GrapheneOS supports with the longest remaining support (Pixel 9a at the time of writing; check GrapheneOS's device list on the day of purchase). |
| Q-14 | Emulator in CI | The FFI memory gate, the write gate and the crash gate on an Android emulator in CI; the capture, lock and Orbot gates on the phones by the owner over `adb`. |

## Not in 1.3

The iPhone client (1.4 — after Q-2, with its own screens issue of DC-05 and the Orbot-for-iOS spike); embedded Arti (S-8 measures it, nothing depends on it); a Play Store listing or Google developer registration (Q-1); the profile on phones (Q-6); booklet generation and printing from a phone (Q-8); circle-key relays on phones; a disguised application; notifications; any update check; F-Droid submission (a later question, after 1.3.0 is reproducible in F-Droid's sense); a tablet layout. The desktop items left open since 1.1 (RM-08 bridges for the session; Tails and Qubes checklists on a signed build; the external reviews, D-19/D-21) stay where they are.

## After 1.3

**1.4 — iPhone**, when the owner has decided Q-2 (an Apple Developer Program account in the owner's or an entity's name, with D-20 amended): a Swift client over the same `aska-ffi`; Orbot for iOS after the `.onion`-through-tunnel spike; snapshot blanking and capture detection (the viewer cannot refuse capture on iOS — it can refuse to show while capture is active); the App Store or TestFlight path with the identity recorded; the iOS guide (what cannot be prevented, what cannot be verified). Then the questions 1.3 defers: Arti in-process on the strength of S-8; booklet generation on a phone if a phone sensor's noise is measured; the Play question.
